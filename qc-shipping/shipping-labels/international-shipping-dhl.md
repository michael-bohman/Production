# International Shipping (DHL)

1. Head to the DHL Express website. If needed, log-in to access the main page. Once there, the "Create a new Shipment" tab should be selected by default.
   1. The sender information may autofill to either mine or Kristen's name. Be sure the address matches our office location.
   2. The recipient address will be what's assigned on the SO and what you will enter in the recipient row marked B.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 122547.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Once entered you will be taken to a general overview of the shipment, displaying more detail on the sender and recipient.&#x20;
   1. If either address needs correction this is the tab to do it.
   2. When you've checked both sides, you may click next.
   3. DHL may at times adjust the address to what's actually in their system. Double check using google maps that it is still correct (Address structure still may look different).

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121614.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. In the shipment details section, be sure to select packages as what's being shipped. We do not ship documents.&#x20;
   1. At times a summarization of what's being shipped will have to be necessary. Be as detailed as you may need in shipment references.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121703.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. In shipment references be sure to include the SO # and finally the value of your shipment. Don't select protect your shipment unless told to.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121714.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. In the custom invoice details, be sure to select "Create Invoice".
   1. Once again type or paste the SO number. Leave the decleration statements unchecked.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121737.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. As long as what's being shipped is below 2,500, it should always be set to yes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121745.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. As we select our own packaging be sure to enter the proper information for each below field. Pull up the excel shipping estimate sheet if needed (Ex: Reflectance panels).
   1. See below for the shipping estimate spreadsheet.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121833.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 131507.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Be sure to select the "847626849" account as we have two. As for the other two columns, have them situationally assigned to either sender/receiver pays and the payment being to "Bill to account"
   1. As for the terms of trade there's an extended list of options. For this case CPT (Carriage Paid To) was selected. CPT transfers the risk of goods to the buyer once the contracted carrier picks up the shipment.&#x20;
   2. The one I most use is DAP (Delivered at Place) which transfers risk to the buyer once the items are at their disposal.&#x20;
   3. Link: [https://mydhl.express.dhl/us/en/shipment-settings/terms-of-trade-definitions.html](https://mydhl.express.dhl/us/en/shipment-settings/terms-of-trade-definitions.html)&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121921.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Next, comes the priority of the shipment. Depending on how much the buyer paid, pick the earliest delivery date.
   1. In this case it would all be delivered the same day, so the options are small but if possible I typically avoid "end of day".

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 121946.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10. In this section, you'll type your name and your title at Sentera. As well as your written signature you can upload to DHL.
    1. However, You can always just use my name and title as well as this file containing my signature.

{% file src="../../.gitbook/assets/Signature.png" %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 122004.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

11. If a pickup has yet to be scheduled, select yes. Then designate the time to 2PM - 6PM and make sure someone will be in the office for it if you leave.
    1. For where the courier should pickup select other. In the row below it make sure to type "Door down the hallway".
    2. Be sure you have typed in the total pick up weight and that the pickup address is correct.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 122042.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

12. Unless told to, you don't need a return label.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 122053.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

13. Finally, comes the total pricing after reading over the information click accept and continue.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-02 122108.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
