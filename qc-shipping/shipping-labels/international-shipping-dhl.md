# International Shipping (DHL)

1. Head to the DHL Express website. If need be then log-in to be taken to the main page. Once there, the "Create a new Shipment" tab should be selected by default(underlined in blue.

* The sender information should autofill to your name (or mine/Kristen) and our business address.
* The recipient address will be what's assigned on the SO and what you will enter in the recipient row marked B.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-09-09 092547.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Once entered you will be taken to a general overview of the shipment, including more detail on the sender and recipient.

* If either address needs correction this is the tab to do it.
* When you've checked both sides you may click next.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-09-09 151705.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. In the shipment details section, be sure to select packages as what's being shipped. We do not do any sort of documents.

* Afterwards, a summarization of what's being shipped will have to be given. Be as detailed as you may need. In shipment refrences.
* In shipment refrences be sure to include the SO# and finally the value of your shipment. NEver select protect your shipment unless told to.
