## Notes:

This guide doesn't completely finish the camera pod. It is finished in the full assembly guide. The things that are missing from this guide are: securing the dual comms board and attaching the cover to the pod.



## Guide:

1. Apply thermal tape to both sides of the camera mount as shown

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9173.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9174.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. Connect the cables to the cameras. Note: The **longer** cables pair with the **front** camera (Multispectral)

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9327.jpeg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9328.jpeg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

3. Feed the wires through the opening of the mount. Route them to avoid pinching.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9186.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Secure the cameras to the mount using 6 screws (3 per camera) (<mark style="color:yellow;">96640A298 2-56 x 7/16" Flat Head, Black</mark>)

{% hint style="info" %}
NOTE: Make sure the Thermal Pro camera is positioned on the back-side (pictured left). Both plugs on the back of the cameras should face forward and the longer lenses should be on the inside of the system, next to each other.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9329.jpeg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Secure the cable to the front camera using the clip and screw.
   1. Clip: 25422-00
   2. Screw: <mark style="color:yellow;">91255A076 2-56 x 3/16" Button Phillips Black</mark>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9187 - Copy.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Connect the cables to the 22139 Dual Comms board as shown. The Primary camera (rear: Thermal Pro) plugs into the camera 1 positions.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9187.jpg" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Secure the Gimbal Pivot bracket to the outside of the pod using 4 screws:
   1. <mark style="color:yellow;">2 x 91698A302 M3x6mm Flat head black</mark> into the pod
   2. <mark style="color:yellow;">2 x 91253A076 2-56 x 5/32" Flat head black</mark> into the cameras
   3. It is easiest to start the M3x6 screws first, then start the 2-56 screws. then tighten everything at the end.

{% hint style="info" %}
Note: orient the bracket so that the shoulder screw hole is on the forward side (left)
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_9331.jpeg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



7. Do not secure the dual comms board to the camera pod or the cover over it until after the green micro coax cable is connected in the full assembly guide.
