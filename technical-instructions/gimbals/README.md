## Notes

1\. When fastening screws into the motors…

a.       Use a SMALL amount of Loctite. If too much is used, it will seep into the motor and cause jamming/stalling. See picture for correct amount of Loctite.

i. Dip into Loctite, the dab both the bottom and sides of the threads on a paper towel.

b.       First, fasten any screw and then fasten the one furthest from it. This helps to seat the motor evenly.
