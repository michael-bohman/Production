# 📷 Astro Legs Assembly

## Guide

### T-Joint Assembly&#x20;

1\. Place the small carbon tube (.57 diameter) into jig as shown in the picture. Use a black sharpie and mark the carbon tube about 1cm away from the end of the jig. This is where the T joint will be placed later.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (4) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt="" width="290"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Using the Araldite gun squeeze Araldite onto a mixing tray. Wipe off any excessive Araldite from the tip of the gun with a blue cleaning paper to prevent it getting stuck for future use. Use a stirring stick, mix Araldite until the solution is completely mixed as shown:

{% columns %}
{% column %}
<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2288.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2289.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

3. Apply Araldite around the carbon tube in between the markings. Make sure you don't get Araldite anywhere outside of the markings as shown:&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2291.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Slide the T-Joint onto the carbon tube.&#x20;
5. Using the jigs. Make sure that the T joint is now aligned and centered.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/20261002_093802.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Wipe off Araldite with Acetone and blue cleaning paper.There cannot be any Araldite visible outside of the T-Joint.&#x20;

{% hint style="danger" %}
If there is any Araldite present outside of the T-Joint when assembly is finished, the legs will not pass shipping QC. Pay attention to this step and throughly complete.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

6. Set assembly aside as it takes 12 hours for aralidite to cure. Proceed to the next steps while waiting for the cure time.&#x20;

### Cap/Pin Install

1. Grab the big carbon tube (.695 diameter). Insert cam follower into the top of the carbon tube and allign the holes. If the holes don't align, then there is a defect with the tube. You will need another tube to replace the defective one.&#x20;
2. Use a hammer and insert the <mark style="color:yellow;">3/4” pin (92373A147)</mark> halfway into the hole as shown:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (10) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. If the pin is not going in straight, you can use a pliers to twist/bend slightly it in the opposite trending direction.&#x20;
9. Using the vice, suppress the pin into the carbon tube for a flush look as shown below:

### Carbon Tube Install

1. Apply Araldite to the open section of the T joint as shown:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (15) (1).png" alt="" width="328"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Insert the big carbon tube (.697" diameter) into the top of the T joint until the holes are aligned. If the holes don't align, report defect to someone immediately.&#x20;
3. Next insert the <mark style="color:yellow;">7/8” pin (92373A148)</mark> into the hole with a hammer halfway as shown below:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (16) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Use the vice to suppress the pin it into place, make sure the pin is not protruding for a flush and clean look. As shown below:

### Cam Lock Bracket

1. Using the wire cutter, clip one side of the torsion spring to <mark style="color:blue;">1/32"</mark> on a ruler. To be sure please use a google image as reference. <mark style="color:$danger;">(Replace picture, too blurry)</mark>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2487.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Fit the spring into the cam lever. The long side of spring should be pointing out as shown. If the spring doesn't fit, slowly trim it down until it does. Confirm the spring doesn't fall out of the lever and is nicely secured.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2486.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Dip <mark style="color:yellow;">shoulder screw (90278A733)</mark> into Loctite. Wipe off any excessive Loctite before inserting the into cam lock bracket.  <mark style="color:yellow;">(Replace left side picture)</mark>
4. Use your 2MM Hex to install and torque it to 40.0 in-oz.&#x20;

5. Using a wire cutter, trim the extruding tip of the long spring.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/2485.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
