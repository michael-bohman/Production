# 🚧 4-B Antenna Assembly

## Things to Change

leave some slack on GPS board

## Materials Required

| Araldite | Paper Towels | Isopropyl Alcohol |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| Loctite  | sharp knife  |                   |
|          |              |                   |

## Notes

This assembly requires an overnight cure time. Plan two days to complete.



## Prep

1. Slightly Chamfer the edges of the nubs on the carbon tubes with a sharp knife. This will make it fit better in the other parts.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8265.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



## Drawing

The drawing for this assembly can be found here: [#pixelscout-v4-antenna-greater-than-21284](../../../space-and-general/drawings.md#pixelscout-v4-antenna-greater-than-21284 "mention")



## Guide

1. Apply araldite to the inside of the holes in the center piece. Do not be afraid to apply too much as it can be cleaned later.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8266.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Insert the carbon tube into the center piece, lining up the alignment tab. Slide it all the way in.
   1. It helps to twist the tube to make sure the araldite covers everything.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8267.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Clean any excess araldite from the inside of the tube to make sure it won't obstruct any wires passing through the tube.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8268.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>This is an excess amount of araldite.</p></figcaption></figure>

4. In the same way, apply araldite to the inside of the tube on the antenna mount

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8270.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. In the same way, attach the antenna mount to the other end of the carbon tube. Match up the alignment tabs in the slots.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8271.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Repeat steps 1-5 on the other side.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_8273.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Wait Overnight for the Araldite to cure.
8. Place 5 light tubes on the center board, inserting the small pins into the holes.
