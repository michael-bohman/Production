# Case Packing (Needs Pictures)

## Notes

* The BOM has a complete list of all the items required to be placed in the case. The following serves as a guide as to where and how they should be organized.
* Cases are delicate and will scratch/smudge VERY easily. To avoid this, keep the case inside the box it came in until final packing/qc. WAIT TO PACK UNTIL THE VERY END.
*



## Test Flight Packing

When flying systems for calibration or verification, use these steps for packing the case.

1. Use the production cases marked with orange tape.
2. Place the gimbal in the leftmost area.
3. Place the antenna assembly in the lid foam.
   1. Close the clasps before inserting.
   2. Secure using the orange Velcro straps.
   3. Tuck the cable into the slot cut in the foam and store the connector in the oval hole.
4. Ensure both a small and long USB-C cable marked 'Production' with orange tape are in the case.
5. Assemble and place the tripod into the large, long slot in the case foam.
   1. Attach the clamp high up (\~1.5" from the top) on the leg between the locking screw and hand crank.
   2. Screw the Ground module onto the clamp.
   3. Apply a liberal amount of Loctite to the mounting screw on the top of the tripod.
   4. Screw on the adapter and tighten using vice grips and paper towels.
   5. screw the emlid base station on top of the tripod.



## Final Packing

After flying the validation flight, use these instructions for packing the final case that is being shipped

1. Place the 3 main components in the same locations as the test flight packing
   1. Place the RJ45 (ethernet) plugs into the ports on the gimbal and antenna.
