# ✅ Reflectance Panel QC

## Notes

QC must be performed by another person who did NOT complete the build

## QC Steps

1. Put on gloves.
2. Open a reflectance panel, inspect the reflectance panel for any nicks, gouges, discoloration, etc.
   1. If any damage is present, put aside and mark as 'Do Not Ship'.&#x20;
      1. consult others for ship-ability
   2. Confirm stickers are correctly installed

> <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_092536.jpg" alt="" data-size="original">    ![](../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_092549.jpg)
>
>

3. Select the next number sticker. Temporarily stick it on the top edge of the open panel as shown, ensuring it is not covering any part of the reflectance panel.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Only use the stickers in the 'Reflectance Panel' Bin. Please place the sticker roll back in the correct bin.&#x20;

<p align="center"><strong>DO NOT USE THE ASTRO BIN STICKER</strong></p>
{% endhint %}

4. Locate the 'Small' light box in the room to take a photo of the Reflectance Panel:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_092631.jpg" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



* The panel number is visible, and 3M tape should NOT be visible.
* The entire reflectance panel is in view
* Every picture is visible with NO blur.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_092759.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Close the container with the Kimwipe tissue paper placed back in between the two halves of the container with an even overhang.
6. Place the sticker on the back of the case, position it in the lower right-hand corner.
   1. "lower" means the bottom right edge next to the latches.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7923.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Place the container into an S-9482 Poly Bag and seal with the Uline Sealer.
   1.  Sealer should be set to 3. Place the container in the bag sticker side first so that the sticker is furthest from the excess material



       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_093037.jpg" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
8. Grab a folded "Sentera Documentation" and staple it on the sides of the bag to keep it secured.&#x20;

> ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (261).png>)     ![](../../.gitbook/assets/20260805_093135.jpg)

9. Upload photos to Taurus with the panel serial number as the photo name.
   1. \\\taurus\Production\Systems & Kits\21226-00 — Reflectance Panel.
   2. Photo naming convention: "Panel XXXX" where XXXX is the serial number on the sticker.
