# 65R Assembly

## Required Items

* Thermal Paste
* Mixing Stick
* Clean Paper Towels
* Loctite
* Kapton Tape

## Notes

Full assembly steps are shown. If programming and/or focusing will occur immediately, omit some steps (will figure out which ones later)

## Assembly Instructions

1. Remove the standoffs and screws from the MPSoC module board. Discard screws and standoffs.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6767.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Apply Thermal Paste to the processor on the board.
   1. Prioritize height rather than coverage to ensure the thermal paste contacts both surfaces when assembled.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6772.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Place the heat sink on the MPSoC module board, ensuring it is aligned properly. Secure with 4 screws (#22: 93085A013) and Loctite.
   1. After tightening, make sure the thermal paste has sufficient coverage of the processor.

5. Apply thermal paste to the TE0820 baseboard.
   1. Be careful with the amount and placement to ensure it will not enter any of the connectors of the board.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6779.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Carefully place the heat diffuser on the baseboard.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6780.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Apply thermal paste to the top of the heat diffuser.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6781.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Insert the NVME SSD into the slot on the board. Secure it to the board using a s crew (#24: 91772A065), 2 washers (#21: 90295A322), and Loctite.
   1. Note: Ensure the 2 washers are between the NVME SSD and the board, and NOT against the screw head.
   2. Note: While tightening, ensure the metal tab stays in place. You may temporarily place the board and heatsink on top of the connectors without connecting them to visualize the correct location of the metal tab.

9. Carefully connect the two boards via the 3 connectors on the top/bottom of them
   1. Note: Make sure even pressure is applied to ensure the 3 connectors are connected evenly/simultaneously.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6786.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10. Connect the heat diffuser press which secures the heat diffuser using 2 screws (#24: 91772A065) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6788.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

11. Connect the USB-C and external connection board to the baseboard and secure with 2 screws (#24: 91772A065) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6792 (1).jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

12. Prepare the fan.
    1. Cut the wires of the fan to \~1.75-2".
    2. Strip the wires and crimp a JST crimp on either end.
    3. Insert the crimps into a 2-wire JST crimp housing. Ensure the ground wire is in slot 1 (the one closest to the triangle).

13. Plug the fan into the board.
    1. Note: if programming will be performed immediately, temporarily secure the fan using a single screw (#23: 91249A060) and Omit Step 19. Otherwise, don't use any screws.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7784.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

14. Place the LED diffusers into the housing. Make sure there is and audible click and they are secured in place.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6797.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

15. Remove the film from the sensor on the imager board and place the board into the housing face down. Secure with 4 screws (#22: 93085A013) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6799.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

16. Turn the housing over and place the lens on the other side. Secure with 2 screws (#25: 93085A621) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_6800.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

17. Carefully attach the stack to the sensor board, making sure it connects evenly on each side.
    1. It may be easiest to place the sensor board lens-side down and place the board stack on top.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7460.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

18. Place the pushbutton in its spot making sure it is oriented the correct way.
    1. The long tab should be facing the heat sink.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7462.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

19. Attach the rear cover using 7 screws (#23: 91249A060) and Loctite.
    1. Note: If programming will be performed immediately, wait to install the cover. In the meantime, secure the stack using 2 screws (#32: 91249A050) across from each other.
