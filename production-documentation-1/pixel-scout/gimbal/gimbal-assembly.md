# Gimbal Assembly

## Materials Needed

In addition to everything listed on the build sheet. You will need:

* Loctite
* Paper Towel

## Preparation Steps

* Mark the JST connectors on the ROLL motor and encoders using a black sharpie.
  * This will help later when attaching the mounting stack.
  * Note: The dash number correlates to the motor position
    * -00: Pitch Motor/Encoder
    * -01: Roll Motor/Encoder

### Attach the Pitch Motor and Encoder

1. Feed the wires through the encoder casing and pitch motor as shown.
   1. Leave about 7.5" of the micro coax cable on the inside of the pitch motor.
   2. Feed the motor cable through the hole in the encoder case and route it alongside the micro coax cable.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7437.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7438.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. Attach the pitch encoder to the back of the pitch motor
   1. Fasten using 4 screws (UNKNOWN NUMBER) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7440.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Attach the combined pitch motor/encoder to the right arm.
   1. Ensure the wires are not pinched or creased.
   2. Fasten using 4 screws (91698A242) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7385.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Feed the pitch motor and micro coax wires through the roll motor casing.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7442.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Attach the roll motor and encoder

1. Feed the pitch motor and micro coax cables through the center of the roll motor.
   1. Feed the roll motor cable through first as it will not fit if the micro coax is already in the motor.
   2. A hex screw driver may be needed to push the JST connector through the motor.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7386.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Place the motor into the casing.
   1. Secure using 4 screws (91698A242) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7443.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Place the cap over the center joint.
   1. Make sure none of the wires are pinched or creased.
   2. Secure using 3 screws (91698A200) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7444.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Push the wires as far back as possible into the inside of the arm.
   1. This is to make sure the wires will have sufficient length during operation and won't interfere with anything.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7389.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Feed all the wires through the roll encoder casing.
   1. There Should be 4 cables:
      1. Micro Coax
         1. Pitch Motor
         2. Roll Motor
         3. Roll Encoder

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7391.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Attach the roll encoder to the back of the motor.
   1. Make sure the wires' resting point is upward.
   2. Secure using 2 screws (UNKNOWN NUMBER) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7392.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Attach the back casing

1. Feed all 4 cables through the hole in the center.
2. Orient so that the slot above the center hole is pointing upward.
3. secure with 4 screws (91698A242) and Loctite.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_7398.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

### Perform checks

Ensure:

* There is no binding when turning any of the motors.
* There are no pinched wires.
* There is about 7.5" of micro coax cabling protruding from the pitch motor.
